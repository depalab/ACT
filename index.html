01 What we do

Engineering teams are drowning in toil.
We replace it with leverage.

We are not a generic consultancy and we don't sell hours. ACT partners with software and operations teams to identify the highest-friction parts of their workflow — then replaces them with AI-assisted systems that compound over time.

/01

Diagnose

We sit alongside your engineers for one to two weeks, instrument the work, and produce a friction map: where minutes leak, where context is lost, where AI can meaningfully intervene.

  • Workflow shadowing
  • Toil & cycle-time audit
  • Opportunity ranking
/02

Design

We design the smallest possible AI-assisted system that solves the real bottleneck — not the buzzword.

/03

Build

From copilots and CLIs to full internal platforms — shipped iteratively, tested in production.

/04

Operate

AI systems decay. Prompts drift. Models change. We stay on as your AI ops partner — evaluations, observability, and continuous improvement so the leverage compounds instead of evaporating.

  • Eval harnesses & regression suites
  • Cost & latency monitoring
  • Model & prompt upgrades

03 Process

From first call to shipped system — in weeks, not quarters.

  1. Week 0

    Discovery

    A 45-minute call. We listen. You walk away with a written summary of the highest-leverage opportunity we see, whether or not you hire us.

  2. Week 1–2

    Embed & Diagnose

    We shadow your team, measure the toil, and produce a written diagnosis with ranked opportunities and rough effort estimates.

  3. Week 3

    Prototype

    The first working prototype lands in production-shaped form — not a slide deck. Real engineers use it, real metrics move.

  4. Week 4–8

    Build & Iterate

    We ship weekly. You see progress weekly. Scope adjusts based on what's actually working.

  5. Ongoing

    Operate

    We stay on as your AI ops partner. Evals, observability, prompt & model upgrades. The leverage compounds instead of decaying.
